GYEOL female transport models (models/female-transport/)

Male-derived structures carried into the NIH Human Reference Atlas female body
(united-female v1.10) by one fitted registration field, and the new positions
of the 180 male-derived ("borrowed") bones placed by the same field.

GYEOL modifications: every vertex moved by the registration field (articulated
pre-pose, residual cubic B-spline deformation, skin layer); normals recomputed;
Draco re-encoding (16-bit positions). Selection rules, fitting inputs and
measurements: data/catalog/female-transport.json,
data/catalog/female-transport-fit.json,
docs/anatomy-alignment/FEMALE_TRANSPORT.md. Reproduction:
scripts/female-transport/ and scripts/build-female-transport.mjs.
These modified files are shared under the same licences.

The geometry is a male-derived educational approximation placed in a female
body; it is not female anatomy and not clinical registration.
