GYEOL modifications: converted 11 published binary NIfTI masks to surfaces
with Lewiner marching cubes at level 0.5; converted RAS millimeters to
left/superior/anterior meters; applied one common scene translation;
calculated normals, quantized normals to int16, packed and gzip-compressed.
No new image segmentation, shape warping or HRA registration is applied.
Display colors and Korean labels are editorial adaptations.

This separate female CT reference is not the same donor as the HRA overview.
Esophagus and back musculature are truncated by the CT field of view.
Source voxel spacing is 1.5mm. This is not a complete female anatomy atlas,
not a clinically validated registration, and not for diagnosis or procedures.
The source authors and institutions have not endorsed or validated this app.

Source masks, selected-case metadata and per-file hashes are retained in
data/female-ct/s0255/ and data/catalog/female-detail-source.json.
The full 23.6GB archive was not downloaded or MD5-verified; selected entries
were checked against ZIP CRC32 and stored with SHA-256 fingerprints.
