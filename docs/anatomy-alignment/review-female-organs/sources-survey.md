- WHO Regional Office for the Western Pacific. WHO Standard Acupuncture Point Locations in the Western Pacific Region. 2008 (2009 reprint). Read from a public reproduction: medbox PDF, 258 pp, SHA-256 c9d4095383c636f85e61b5bc5d7631a658308c0336e8f691b0eb29f517d5bf5b. The official IRIS handle returns 403 to WebFetch. — https://iris.who.int/handle/10665/353407 (reproduction: https://medbox.org/dl/627a4de115110145a1723f64)
  - Introduction: 'Only by using the Equal Proportional Measurement method ... can the proper location of acupuncture points be established for all population groups and individuals.' p.11: 'The 4th intercostal space: at the same level as the nipples in males.' p.12 table: nipples 8 B-cun apart; suprasternal notch to xiphisternal junction 9; xiphisternal to umbilicus 8; umbilicus to superior border of pubic symphysis 5; axillary fold to cubital crease 9; cubital crease to wrist 12. Pubic symphysis to patella base 18 is on p.13. p.13 lists 'differences in surface landmarks between different genders and individuals' among the note types. Female-specific notes: ST17 p.54 ('In males, the centre of the nipple is located in the fourth intercostal space'); ST18 p.54 ('In females, ST18 is located at the midpoint of the inferior crease of the breast'); GB24 p.183 (in females, midclavicular line and 7th intercostal space); LR14 p.202 (in females, midclavicular line and 6th intercostal space); CV1 p.220 (posterior commissure of labia majora in females).
  - 관련성: Gives an explicit, sex-aware rule set for building female points from the female body's own landmarks, instead of carrying male anchors across. A read-only probe of public/models/acupoint-anchors-female.json against the cached female scene (.cache/female-transport/female-scene, the frame used by scripts/build-female-anchors.mjs) shows the current approximations break these rules. ST17 lands 63.5 mm (R) and 94.0 mm (L) from the female nipple centroids, 56–83 mm too high, with a 27 mm left/right height asymmetry; the nipples themselves differ by under 1 mm. ST18 sits above the nipple, 84–104 mm from the lowest breast-fat point below the nipple, where WHO places it at the inframammary crease. CV8 is about 40 mm above the skin's midline umbilical dimple (0.974 m), and CV2 is 37 mm above the top of the female pubic symphysis (0.797 m). These are geometric checks, not expert review.
- Lv PR, Qian ZY, Zhao L, Shen XY, Deng HP. [Bone proportional measurement on the chest and abdomen among 101 young females]. Zhongguo Zhen Jiu. 2022;42(3):287-289. doi:10.13703/j.0255-2930.20210204-k0005. PMID 35272406. — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35272406/
  - In 101 young women (height/75 converted to cun): the distance between the nipples was 8 cun and between the coracoid processes 12 cun, matching the 2006 Chinese national standard. Umbilicus to superior pubic symphysis was 6.5 cun (the Lingshu value), not 5. Suprasternal notch to xiphisternal junction was under 9 cun; the authors propose using the midpoint of the xiphoid instead.
  - 관련성: The only located study measuring proportional cun specifically in women. It supports keeping 8 B-cun between the nipples for width. It also shows that segment-to-height ratios differ in the lower abdomen. B-cun is defined within each segment, so locating on the woman's own umbilicus and pubis adapts automatically.
- Kang Y, Park J, Lee T, Yang G, Chae H, Lee B. Analysis of the Bone Proportional Method for Determining Acupoints in the Upper and Lower Abdominal Region in Males and Females.   - 30 men and 30 women aged 20–40, tape and caliper, cun derived from height. Upper abdomen: women 8.40 ± 0.97 cun (p=0.029 against WHO 8), men 8.02. Lower abdomen: women 7.88 ± 1.10 cun, men 6.33 ± 0.93 (both p<0.001 against WHO 5). Raw lower-abdomen length: women 168.3 mm, men 148.1 mm.
  - 관련성: Direct evidence of sex differences in lower-abdominal proportions. The HRA female skin's umbilicus-to-pubic-symphysis distance measured in the probe (~177 mm) is close to the female mean here, which supports deriving the CV/KI/ST/SP abdominal points from the female's own umbilicus and pubis.

  - In 47 Korean women measured by DXA, 1 cun by the finger (directional) method differed significantly from 1 cun by the proportional method. Proportional is the more dependable of the two.
  - 관련성: Supports using B-cun proportions on female landmarks, not finger-cun or absolute millimetre offsets carried from the male.

## 권고(원문)

Summary (checked 2026-10-25): no open, redistributable female source is newer or more complete than what the app already holds. HRA united-female v1.10 (HRA v2.5) is the latest female body; CDN v1.11/v2.0 return 404. HRA blood-vasculature-female v1.3 is the latest vasculature and only covers vessels around organs. HRA has no female ribs, skull, arm or foot bones, whole-body muscles or peripheral nerves; the v2.5 'skeleton' and 'muscular system' additions are naming tables, not meshes. Every other female candidate is either a different body or carries a non-commercial, ShareAlike or permission-only license: Visible Korean, ICRP-145, IT'IS Ella, SMPL-X/STAR, NIH 3D pelvis 3DPX-015843, Duke breast MRI, BoneHub VSD. Z-Anatomy and BodyParts3D have no female content.

1) Female-specific organs. The data are already loaded; the fix is in display.
- Organ colour: give the reproductive structures their own colour token instead of the shared organ colour #a94d60. They are vagina HRAF0406, cervicovaginal junction 0407, uterine tube 0408–0415, uterovesical pouch 0416, ligaments 0417–0431 and uterus 0434–0443 (ovaries 0432/0433 included).
- Isolation: add a '여성 생식기관' comparison bundle that goes through src/view-state.ts, with an anterior-inferior or sagittal framing, so the descending/sigmoid colon (HRAF0521/0525) and bladder (HRAF0609) stop hiding it.
- Breast internals: move mammary lobes 0008/0018, lactiferous ducts 0009/0017, sinuses 0010/0016 and suspensory ligaments 0011/0019 from the 'skin' peel layer into a breast/glandular group that peel and '장기만 보기' keep visible. Areola, nipple and fat can stay with skin.
- Provenance: state that the breast internals are HRA's sculpted reference model (atlas-based, reviewed), not the donor's segmented tissue.
- Labels: add Korean labels while keeping the English names and FMA/UBERON IDs from the HRA crosswalk (e.g. FMA:57991, UBERON:0018140, UBERON:0015134, FMA:73119).
- Size note: say that the uterus and ovaries are small because the VHF donor was 59 and postmenopausal.
- Hash pin: data/female-atlas-structures.json (sha256 4affd375…) is pinned in many docs/anatomy-alignment audit JSONs. Prefer a display-side override map, or regenerate the file and re-run those audits.
- Modesty: keep the existing hide rule unchanged.

2) Female acupoint approximations. This is the highest-value fix and needs no new data. Replace the transported male anchors with points derived by WHO 2008 B-cun rules on the female's own HRA landmarks:
- Landmarks available: nipples HRAF0006/0014, umbilicus (skin dimple at 0.974 m, matching adipose patch HRAF0000), pubic symphysis (HRA v1.5 pubis), sternum/manubrium/xiphoid, C7–S vertebrae and PSIS, patella, tibia/fibula malleoli, skin creases.
- Apply WHO's female notes: ST17 at the nipple centre, ST18 at the midpoint of the inframammary crease, GB24/LR14 on the midclavicular line at the 7th/6th intercostal space, CV1 at the posterior commissure. Do not use the male rule 'nipple = 4th intercostal space'.
- Evidence the current anchors are wrong: ST17 is 63.5/94.0 mm from the female nipples, ST18 sits above the nipple, CV8 is about 40 mm high and CV2 37 mm high.
- Kang 2019 and Lv 2022 show female abdominal proportions differ from male, which is exactly what deriving from her own landmarks absorbs.
- Points that depend on borrowed bones (intercostal spaces, scapula, skull, arm, hand, foot) stay flagged '근사' until those bones are female.
- Keep the 'unreviewed, hidden by default' status. Show points as approximate locations (Molsberger 2012; Godson 2019). Make no needling-depth or efficacy claims.

The outputs would be candidates only and must go through the same holdout, registration and expert-review gates as the MOOSE output. Do not re-propose the rejected transport methods.
